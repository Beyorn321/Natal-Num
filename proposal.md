Name: Natal Nums
Concept: A puzzle solving, text based game, based upon Dudo or liars dice. It is a game that should take around 3-5 hours and requiers critical thinking, trial and error, and information gathering to be able to be played.
Story: Two aliens named Xphee and Shtirk, travel across endless space for a unknown reason until their ship suddenly crashes on another planet. These aliens now have to find the ship parts that have scatter across the world from the crash, through hostile enemies that want to take them down for their meat, so they can fufill their unknown reason.
Theme: This story explores creatures that are uncanny, satire, and can bring horror throughout their adventure.
Audience: The audience of this game would be people who would like saitre, puzzle solving, and uncanniness in their games.
Group: Me, I, Myself, and Bjorn.
